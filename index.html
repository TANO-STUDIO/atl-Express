Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where does Altexpress operate?
Altexpress operates in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, China, Australia, throughout Europe, and worldwide — with nationwide US coverage, 24–48h delivery across the EU, and shipping to 200+ countries.
Does Altexpress offer eFulfillment for online stores?
Yes. We provide a complete eFulfillment stack — OMS, WMS, LMS, DMS and more — purpose-built for European eCommerce.
What payment methods does Altexpress accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards, bank transfers, and digital wallets for seamless payment across our global network.
How does the MyAltexpress platform help my business?
MyAltexpress automates AWB creation, courier comparison, tracking, notifications and invoicing in a single dashboard.
Can I connect Altexpress with my Shopify or WooCommerce store?
Yes, official Shopify and WooCommerce integrations sync orders, statuses and tracking automatically.
