Our Story (So Far)

Born in 2015. Built for borderless commerce.

AltExpress was born out of a simple vision: to make online commerce easier, faster, and borderless. From our first warehouse in Bucharest to our growing global network with hubs in the US, Europe, and Asia, we've shipped over 1.5 million orders for more than 3,000 businesses and 33,000 direct customers. Today, we're proud to be the trusted fulfillment partner for brands reaching customers across the world.
